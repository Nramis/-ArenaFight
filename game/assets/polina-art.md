# Polina art

Four transparent RGBA 1536 × 1024 sprite atlases generated using imagegen with the user-supplied portrait as the identity reference. Outfit: cream T-shirt, white trousers, brown belt, white sneakers, long light brown center-parted hair. Crisp outlined cel shaded style, nonsexual fully clothed athletic poses, side view facing right, four columns and two rows, full body inside cells, no lettering or scenery.

Files: motion/polina.png, transitions/polina.png, postures/polina.png, polina-specials.png. The motion atlas is also copied unchanged to dist/portraits/polina.png.

Pose briefs: motion uses guard, two strides, punch, kick, jump, hurt and crouch. Transitions use two passing strides, punch windup/retraction, kick chamber/retraction, ground knockdown and get-up. Postures use half-squat, crouching jab, low kick, sweep, aerial windup/punch/kick and landing. Specials use two palms-together swimming preparation poses, two kneeling requests, a hand heart, middle-finger taunt, thumb-to-nose teasing gesture and a teleport palm strike. No sexual gestures or voice recordings.

Source PNGs are unchanged. PolinaAtlas stores component-based UV bounds to isolate limbs extending beyond nominal cells; other atlas metadata stores ground contact anchors. Import settings use WebP compression at 85% quality with alpha preserved.
