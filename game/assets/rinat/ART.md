# Rinat and Paquito generated art

Built-in `image_gen` produced these project-bound assets. Original image outputs were copied without cropping, recoloring, resizing, alpha modification, or other raster edits. `rinat-master.png` uses a second generation to keep all shoes inside the canvas. Every final sheet is RGBA 1448 × 1086 with 32 distinct poses in visual row-major order (8 columns × 4 rows). Generated rows are not exact geometric cells; consume `regions.json`, not regular grid slicing.

`tools/build_rinat_regions.py` analyzes alpha > 128, ignores isolated noise, measures sprite regions, and produces a per-body outline polygon plus foot anchor. Paquito contains two pairs of poses joined by tiny contacts; metadata splits those with a geodesic flood from eroded body cores. Source PNGs remain untouched. Alpha silhouettes contain slight antialiasing, excluded loose edge speckles, and shader-readable polygons. Jump and knockdown sprites intentionally have different body height.

Base pose order (0–31): idle, shifted idle, walk contact, walk passing, walk reverse, crouch, crouch guard, jump ascent, jump apex, landing, punch windup, straight punch, hook, heavy punch, low kick, high kick, crouch jab, uppercut windup, uppercut rise, uppercut followthrough, overhead guard, hit recoil, knockdown, victory, chain windup, chain throw, chain pull, elixir drink, wipe mouth, vape hold, vape exhale, shout.

Master pose order: same first 24 semantic poses in loose drunken-master style; 24–26 shirt/tunic tear sequence, 27–28 singing/shouting gestures, 29 vape hold, 30 exhale, 31 laugh. Paquito substitutes boxing actions in frames 24–31 and does not gain Rinat abilities.

Metadata coordinates are absolute pixels in the original sheet: `rect=[x,y,width,height]`, `anchor=[foot_center_x,bottom_y]`, and `polygon=[[x,y],...]`. `height` is the idle body height used to normalize drawing scale, not a fixed cell height. It requires Pillow, numpy, scipy and matplotlib.
